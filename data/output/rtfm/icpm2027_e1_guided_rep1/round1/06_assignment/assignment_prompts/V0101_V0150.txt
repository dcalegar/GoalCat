The taxonomy was derived by subdividing a declared goal-model axis — every category is traceable to specific goal-model elements, so 'realize' means the narrative satisfies that declared alternative.

=== TAXONOMY CATEGORIES ===
- category_id=timely_payment | name=Resolve via timely payment
  description: Resolution of the fine case through early payment by the offender. Advances the 'Maximize timely fine revenue' softgoal and helps 'Minimize administrative & enforcement cost'. Measured against indicator 'Time to fine dispatch (days)' and 'Average time to case closure (days)'.
- category_id=delinquent_payment | name=Resolve via delinquent payment
  description: Resolution of the fine case through payment after notifications and penalties have been applied. Helps 'Maximize timely fine revenue'. Measured against indicator 'Average time to case closure (days)'.
- category_id=administrative_appeal | name=Resolve via administrative appeal to the Prefecture
  description: Resolution path involving administrative appeal to the Prefecture. Preserves offender's due-process rights while impacting enforcement costs and revenue. Measured against indicator 'Time to appeal filing, Prefecture (days)'.
- category_id=judicial_appeal | name=Resolve via judicial appeal to the Judge
  description: Resolution path involving judicial appeal to the Judge. Strongly preserves due-process rights but hurts enforcement cost and revenue. Measured against indicator 'Time to appeal filing, Judge (days)'.
- category_id=coercive_credit_collection | name=Resolve via coercive credit collection
  description: Resolution path via coercive credit collection for unpaid or unresolved fines. Helps revenue but hurts cost and due-process softgoals. Measured against indicator 'Average time to case closure (days)'.

How to read each line below: one line is one process *variant* — a distinct activity sequence — not one case.
- frequency is how many cases share that sequence, with that count as a share of all cases in the log in parentheses; duration_median is the median case duration for that variant, written compactly on the same scale as the waits inside the narrative ("134d", "3h", "45m"); outcome is the variant's final activity.
- The activity sequence shown after "narrative:" is that of a single representative case (the one whose duration is closest to the variant's median), so a "(+34d)" after an activity is that case's waiting time since the preceding activity, not a variant-wide average. Activities are listed in the order they occurred. A "(handled by resource R)" clause, where present, names the resource that performed that activity in the representative case. A trailing "Rework observed: ..." sentence, where present, is a variant-level count over the variant's own cases, not part of the representative case's sequence.
- Activity labels come from the event log and need not match a category's wording verbatim: a logged activity realizes a category by meaning, not by string equality.
