- category_id=three_way_matched_after_gr | name=3-way matched, invoice recorded after goods receipt
  description: Represents items resolved via 3-way matching where the goods receipt precedes the invoice receipt. This alternative makes a strong positive contribution to preserving value-matching control integrity and is judged against the Time to clearance, 3-way after GR (days) indicator.

  description: Represents items resolved via 3-way matching where the invoice is recorded before the goods receipt occurs. This alternative helps maximize working-capital efficiency and preserve control integrity, and is judged against the Time to clearance, 3-way before GR (days) indicator.

  description: Represents items resolved through a 2-way match where no goods receipt is required. This helps minimize procurement and matching overhead, and is judged against the Time to clearance, 2-way match (days) indicator.

  description: Represents items resolved via consignment stock consumption. This helps minimize procurement and matching overhead, and is judged against exception/rework rates and clearance indicators.
