You are deciding, for each of several process-variant narratives, which single taxonomy category (if any) it realizes.

The taxonomy was derived by subdividing a declared goal-model axis — every category is traceable to specific goal-model elements, so 'realize' means the narrative satisfies that declared alternative.

=== TAXONOMY CATEGORIES ===
- category_id=straight_through_processing | name=Straight-through processing
  description: The invoice is processed and paid with no recorded exception, block, discrepancy correction, or cancellation — the shortest, most direct realization of the process.
- category_id=discrepancy_resolved | name=Discrepancy resolved before payment
  description: A price, quantity, or date discrepancy is detected and corrected on record before the invoice is paid, without a full block or cancellation.
- category_id=delayed_or_blocked_payment | name=Delayed or blocked payment
  description: Payment occurs materially after the invoice's recorded terms, or a payment-block event is on record, without the case ending in a cancellation or reversal.
- category_id=cancelled_or_reversed | name=Cancelled or reversed
  description: The purchase order or invoice is cancelled or reversed on record rather than completed through to payment.

- The activity sequence shown after "narrative:" is that of a single representative case (the one whose duration is closest to the variant's median), so a "(+34d)" after an activity is that case's waiting time since the preceding activity, not a variant-wide average. Activities are listed in the order they occurred. A "(handled by resource R)" clause, where present, names the resource that performed that activity in the representative case. A trailing "Rework observed: ..." sentence, where present, is a variant-level count over the variant's own cases, not part of the representative case's sequence.
- Activity labels come from the event log and need not match a category's wording verbatim: a logged activity realizes a category by meaning, not by string equality.
